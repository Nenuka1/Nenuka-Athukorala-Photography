Behind the Lens

I’m Nenuka Dulaj Athukorala, a passionate photographer from Sri Lanka who loves capturing beautiful moments and turning them into lasting memories.

To me, photography is more than simply capturing an image with a camera. It is about capturing emotions, memories, and stories. From a genuine smile to the quiet beauty of nature, and from the little moments we often overlook to the special moments we never want to forget, I look for a story hidden within every frame.

Through my lens, I get to see the world from a different perspective. I try to capture not only what the eyes can see, but also what the heart can feel. Because I believe a meaningful photograph is a memory that can take us back to a moment, even after time has passed.

My journey in photography is still growing. With every photograph, I continue to learn, explore, experiment, and create my own visual story.

My purpose is simple — to turn ordinary moments into lasting memories and to create photographs that tell stories without the need for words.
